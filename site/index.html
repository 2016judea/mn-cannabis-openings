211 Twin Cities cannabis businesses licensed or about to open

Every dispensary needs guards at the door and every grow needs product moved. These are the seven-county metro's licensed cannabis businesses, plus the Minneapolis dispensaries and grows building out right now. 19 are at or near final inspection, 41 got their state license in the last 90 days, and the state lists a named contact and email for 183.

    Sources, all public: the Minnesota Office of Cannabis Management's licensed-business list (published Oct 12, 2026), and the City of Minneapolis building permit register (pulled Oct 17, 2026).

    How the stage is read. Opening soon: a Minneapolis permit for a dispensary or grow is under construction or at final inspection. Newly licensed: the state license was issued in the last 90 days. Built out, not on the state list: a finished Minneapolis cannabis permit at an address the state list doesn't carry yet. Only Minneapolis publishes its permits, so suburban and Saint Paul rows carry the state license alone. Businesses with no site address on the state list sit at the middle of their city on the map.

    Hemp-edible (LPHE) retailers are left out. From Aidan Jude at Brick & Mortar (aidan@brickandmortar.dev).
